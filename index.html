RESEARCH / 001

J-Space Deliberation Engine

Structured Latent Deliberation for Gemma 4 E4B using Jacobian Lens.

J-Space is an experimental latent reasoning architecture that introduces a structured five-slot workspace into the residual stream. The system organizes internal deliberation into Objective, Hypothesis, Evidence, Critic and Commit before final text generation.

Objective Defines the goal
Hypothesis Candidate solutions
Evidence Relevant signals
Critic Consistency check
Commit Final consolidation
